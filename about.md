## Background

I have a Master's Degree in Computer Science (minor in Biology) from the University of Copenhagen. Wrote my thesis in robotics and AI.

I've worked in the computer games industry for more than 20 years.

I started my games career with working for NDS Denmark (Visionik) for 10 years. Making casual games for the OpenTV platform (in C). I was one of the lead programmers (Software Architect). I also ran a small infrastructure department.

(One of my most important projects at NDS was [WOK](/projects/project-wok/), a tool that cut our feedback loop from minutes to seconds.)

After NDS Denmark closed I joined forces with three colleagues to form Red Zebra Games where we made the game [Raid Leader](/projects/project-raidleader/). We got great reviews for our game.

Since then I've worked many different places, mostly smaller danish indie game studios (eg. BetaDwarf and Triband)

I also worked for Unity in the editor department for half a year.
