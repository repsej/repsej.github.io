## Background

I have a Master's Degree in Computer Science (minor in Biology). Wrote my thesis in robotics and AI.

I've worked in the computer games industry for more than 20 years.

I started my games career with working for NDS-DK (Visionik) for 10 years. Making casual games for the OpenTV platform (in C). I was one of the lead programmers (Software Architect). I also ran a small infrastructure department.

After NDS-DK closed I joined forces with three colleagues in Red Zebra Games where we made the game Raid Leader. We got great reviews for the game.

Since then I've worked many different places, mostly smaller indie game studios (eg. BetaDwarf and Triband)

I also briefly worked for Unity in the editor department.

## Hobbies

I love camping, I read old books, paint, make music and small games in my spare time.
