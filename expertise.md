## Gameplay programming

I was the sole programmer on [Raid Leader](/projects/project-raidleader/) and made about 15 casual games for OpenTV at NDS Denmark. I've also worked on game logic for Minion Masters, WHAT THE GOLF?, and on GoldDigger.

I also do small games in my spare time.  Can't stop making games :)

## Low-level programming

I've always enjoyed the mental puzzle of doing low level programming and really getting the most of the hardware.

I did low-level debugging on Minion Masters at BetaDwarf. At NDS Denmark I wrote about 15 casual games in C - with lots of low level optimizations, I helped port Unity to an interactive television platform and made [WOK](/projects/project-wok/).

## UI programming

I worked on the GUI of Minion Masters and created the Unity client for [TurnHero](/projects/project-turnhero/).

## Tools and workflows

I worked on maintaining and developing the Unity Editor at Unity Technologies, and internal libraries and tools at FRVR. At NDS Denmark I made [WOK](/projects/project-wok/), a PC runtime for OpenTV games, to speed up development and ported the development platform from Unix to Windows/Cygwin.

## Performance optimization

I worked on optimization for Minion Masters at BetaDwarf.

## Languages and tools

- **Languages:** C#, C, C++, JavaScript, TS
- **Engine:** Unity
- **Platforms:** H5, mobiles, OpenTV
