## Gameplay programming

I was the sole programmer on [Raid Leader](/projects/project-raidleader/) and made about 15 casual games for OpenTV at NDS Denmark. I've also worked on game logic for Minion Masters, on WHAT THE GOLF?, and on GoldDigger, a free-to-play JavaScript game with 3.5M monthly active users.

## Low-level programming

I did low-level debugging on Minion Masters at BetaDwarf. At NDS Denmark I helped port Unity to an interactive television platform and made a simulator of the OpenTV middleware.

## UI programming

I worked on the GUI of Minion Masters and created the Unity client for [TurnHero](/projects/project-turnhero/), a generic board games platform.
