## Gameplay programming

I made about 15 casual games for OpenTV at NDS Denmark. I was the sole programmer on [Raid Leader](/projects/project-raidleader/) and I've also worked on game logic for Minion Masters, WHAT THE GOLF?, and GoldDigger.

I also do small games in my spare time. Can't stop making games.

## Prototyping

Instead of endless discussions I've always been a firm believer in just doing a prototype.

---

At NDS Denmark I became a big fan of GameMaker (the old 8.1 version) - and started doing a lot of prototypes with it. I used it both for:

- Prototyping games for clients before starting the real development process.
- Experimenting with eg "one button" games and other experimental and novel game designs.

---

At NDS Denmark, I took the initiative to move the company away from describing games in detail in written contracts and towards using prototypes instead.

The written contracts tended to trigger nitpicking and arguments with customers. Somehow, the wording became more important than the games themselves.

Instead, we started by building a prototype of the game in just a few days using GameMaker 8.1. We would get feedback from the customer, iterate on the prototype, and once we were both happy with it, implement the game in OpenTV. This final implementation normally took around three months.

The result was better games and relationships with our customers.

## Low-level programming

I've always enjoyed the mental puzzle of doing low level programming and really getting the most out of the hardware.

Examples:

- At NDS Denmark I wrote about 15 casual games in C - with lots of low level optimizations,
- I also worked on porting Unity to an interactive television platform and made [WOK](/projects/project-wok/).
- I did low-level debugging on Minion Masters at BetaDwarf.

## Tools and workflows

At NDS Denmark I made [WOK](/projects/project-wok/), a PC runtime for OpenTV games, to speed up development and ported the development platform from Unix to Windows/Cygwin.

I also made and maintained many other tools and libraries for our OpenTV development.

I worked on maintaining and developing the Unity Editor at Unity Technologies, and worked on internal libraries and tools at FRVR.
